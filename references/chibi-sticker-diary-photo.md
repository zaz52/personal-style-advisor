# Q版分身手账照 / Chibi Sticker Diary Photo

Use this template when the user uploads a real-life photo and wants a cute `Q版分身手账照`, `Chibi Sticker Diary Photo`, `Q版小人贴纸`, `手账涂鸦照片`, or social-media diary-style photo enhancement.

## Output Goal

Create one high-completion realistic-photo enhancement that keeps the original real person and scene as the main image, then adds cute chibi mini versions, scrapbook doodles, and handwritten diary notes around the subject.

Recommended aspect ratios:

- Preserve the uploaded photo's original ratio when possible.
- 4:5 or 3:4 for Xiaohongshu / Instagram-style portraits.
- 9:16 for vertical lifestyle stories.
- 1:1 for sticker-like social posts.

## Core Prompt

```text
Please create a high-completion "Q版分身手账照 / Chibi Sticker Diary Photo" based on the user's uploaded real-life photo.

Image A is the user's real photo. Keep the original main subject and original scene as much as possible, and enhance the whole image into a cute chibi-sticker scrapbook diary photo.

Core identity and photography preservation:
- Preserve the real person in the original photo.
- Keep the person's facial features, hairstyle, hair color, clothing, body proportion, pose, lighting, and background environment.
- Do not swap the face.
- Do not obviously change the person's identity.
- Do not over-beautify the person.
- Do not turn the real main subject into an illustration.
- Do not destroy the realistic photography feeling of the original image.

Automatic theme recognition:
Analyze the uploaded photo's scene, person state, outfit, gesture, and atmosphere. Automatically decide the real-life theme, such as work, commuting, daily selfie, casual outfit, shopping, cafe visit, food and drink, home daily life, travel, fitness, sport, study, light lifestyle, or another theme that best matches the photo.

All chibi mini versions, doodles, handwritten notes, icons, and emotions must match the real theme and atmosphere of the photo. Do not add random unrelated actions or text.

Chibi mini versions:
Add 5-8 chibi-style mini versions of the same person around the real main subject. These chibi figures must look like cute mini evolutions of the real person in Image A.

Each chibi mini version must preserve the person's recognizable hairstyle, hair color, clothing colors, accessories, temperament, and key appearance traits, so viewers can immediately tell they are the same person's chibi avatars.

Chibi style requirements:
- cute chibi / kawaii sticker style
- big head, small body
- large expressive eyes
- rich facial expressions
- cute lively actions
- polished digital sticker quality
- unified style across all mini versions
- no mixed illustration styles

Action and emotion design:
Create multiple states, actions, and emotions for the chibi mini versions according to the recognized life theme. Every mini version should have a different action and different emotion. Do not repeat the same standing pose.

Theme examples:
- If the photo is work / office / commuting: laptop, documents, typing, drinking coffee, rushing to work, thinking, cheering, relaxing after work.
- If the photo is daily selfie / outfit: jumping, wink, peace sign, waving, chin-on-hand, carrying a bag, posing, drinking a beverage.
- If the photo is food / drink / cafe / entertainment: eating, drinking milk tea, holding a cup, happy waving, sitting and resting, taking photos, daydreaming with chin on hand.
- If the photo is fitness / sport: lifting dumbbells, squatting, running, stretching, drinking a shaker, cheering, resting, satisfied after sweating.
- If the photo is travel: taking photos, looking at a map, pulling luggage, sitting and enjoying scenery, running, eating local snacks, waving.
- If the photo is home daily life: drinking, curled up sitting, hugging a pillow, relaxing, zoning out, reading, greeting.

The actions must relate to the person's current scene and life state, presenting a small universe of different moods from the same person.

Sticker placement:
Place the chibi mini versions like cute stickers attached to the real photo. Distribute them around the main person, near the image edges, or in blank areas.

Each chibi sticker can include:
- white outline
- soft shadow
- slight floating effect
- sticker-like edge

The overall result should feel like a refined, unified, natural fusion of realistic photo and stickers. Do not cover the real subject's important face or body areas. Do not make the layout too crowded.

Scrapbook doodle elements:
Add hand-drawn doodle elements in blank areas to strengthen the diary/scrapbook feeling and emotional expression.

Possible doodles:
hearts, stars, sparkles, arrows, sketchy lines, underlines, circles, cute icons, speech bubbles, motion marks.

Colors:
- mainly white
- a small amount of pink
- optionally a few coordinated accent colors based on the photo atmosphere

The doodle style should feel like handwritten diary notes, photo annotations, and casual cute decoration. It should be light, natural, playful, not too formal, and not messy.

Handwritten short notes:
Add 5-8 short handwritten-style phrases around the image, based on the photo's theme and the person's state.

The phrases should feel like handwritten notes or small mood slogans: relaxed, cute, lifestyle-oriented, emotional, encouraging, or playful. Do not write long explanations. Do not make the text too formal.

The copy direction can lean toward:
- lifestyle feeling
- relaxed mood
- energetic mood
- encouragement
- cuteness
- workday daily life
- fitness check-in
- travel happiness

Typography:
Use a handwritten style. Main text color should be white. Highlight key words with pink or a subtle theme color. Some phrases can include hand-drawn underlines or tiny decorations.

Composition:
Use the structure: central real main subject + surrounding chibi mini versions + doodles and short notes filling blank areas.

The visual focus must still be the real person. Chibi stickers and handwritten elements enhance fun and storytelling. The image should be rich, layered, and worth looking at repeatedly, while still clean, organized, and not a chaotic collage.

Overall style keywords:
realistic photo enhancement, chibi mini versions, kawaii sticker style, scrapbook doodles, handwritten notes, lifestyle diary aesthetic, playful and polished, soft lighting, cute but clean, high detail, social-media-friendly.

Final effect:
The final image should look like a polished social-media lifestyle scrapbook diary photo:
- the real person is natural and clear
- chibi mini versions look like cute sticker extensions of the same person
- actions are diverse and emotions are varied
- doodles and handwritten short phrases are naturally distributed
- the overall feeling is refined, relaxed, cute, and content-rich

Avoid:
- garbled text
- inconsistent chibi characters
- chibi avatars that do not look like the person
- changing the person's real identity
- over-beautifying the real subject
- turning the original photo into a full illustration
- overcrowding
- cheap collage feeling
- covering the main subject
- actions or copy unrelated to the photo theme
```

## Chinese User-Facing Prompt

```text
请根据用户上传的真人生活照片，创作一张高完成度的「Q版分身手账照 / Chibi Sticker Diary Photo」。

【核心任务】
Image A 是用户上传的真实照片，请在尽量保留原图主体与原始场景的基础上，对整张图片进行“Q版分身手账化增强”。

请保留原图中的真实人物、脸部特征、发型、发色、服装、身材比例、姿势、光线和背景环境，不要换脸，不要明显改变人物身份，不要过度美颜，不要把真人主体变成插画，也不要破坏原图的真实摄影感。

【自动识别与主题判断】
请根据上传照片中的场景、人物状态、穿搭和动作，自动判断这张照片更偏向哪种生活主题，例如：工作 / 上班 / 通勤 / 日常自拍 / 休闲穿搭 / 购物逛街 / 咖啡探店 / 吃喝玩乐 / 居家日常 / 旅行 / 健身 / 运动 / 学习 / 轻生活方式等。
整张图的Q版分身动作、手绘元素和手写文案，都应围绕照片本身的真实主题与氛围自动匹配，而不是随机添加。

【Q版分身角色】
围绕真人主体添加 5-8 个同一人物的Q版迷你分身（chibi-style mini versions）。
这些Q版分身必须是从真人主角的形象演化出来的可爱迷你版本，保留人物的发型、发色、服装颜色、配饰、气质和主要外形特征，确保一眼就能看出是同一个人的Q版化身。

Q版角色风格要求：cute chibi / kawaii sticker style，大头小身，大眼睛，表情丰富，动作可爱生动，像数字贴纸一样精致，统一风格，不能画风混乱。

【动作与情绪设计】
请根据照片主题，为这些Q版分身自动设计多状态、多动作、多情绪表现。每个分身都要有不同动作和不同情绪，不要重复站姿。

例如：如果照片偏工作/上班：拿电脑、看文件、打字、喝咖啡、赶路、思考、加油打气、下班放松。如果照片偏日常自拍/穿搭：跳跃、wink、比耶、挥手、托腮、拎包、摆pose、喝饮品。如果照片偏吃喝玩乐/探店：吃东西、喝奶茶、举杯、开心挥手、坐着休息、拍照、托腮发呆。如果照片偏健身/运动：举哑铃、深蹲、跑步、拉伸、喝摇摇杯、加油、休息、流汗后满足感。如果照片偏旅行：拍照、看地图、拖行李、坐着看风景、奔跑、吃当地小吃、挥手。如果照片偏居家：喝饮料、窝着坐、抱枕、放松、发呆、看书、打招呼。

总之，动作必须与人物当下场景和生活状态相关，呈现“同一个人不同状态的小宇宙”。

【贴纸呈现方式】
这些Q版分身应像贴在照片上的可爱贴纸，分布在人物四周、画面边缘或留白区域。每个分身可以带有白色描边、柔和阴影、轻微浮起感、贴纸感边缘。整体要有精致、统一、自然的“照片×贴纸融合感”。不要遮挡真人主体的重要面部和身体区域，不要让布局过于拥挤。

【手绘涂鸦元素】
在画面空白区域加入手绘涂鸦元素，增强手账感与情绪表达。可包含 hearts, stars, sparkles, arrows, sketchy lines, underlines, circles, cute icons, speech bubbles, motion marks。
颜色以白色为主，辅以少量粉色，也可以根据照片整体氛围加入少量协调的点缀色。涂鸦风格应像手账笔记、照片手绘标注、随手写画的可爱装饰，轻松、自然、有趣，不要太正式，也不要太凌乱。

【手写文案】
根据照片主题与人物状态，在画面四周加入 5-8 句简短的手写风短句。这些短句应像手写笔记或状态标语，轻松、可爱、有生活感、情绪感或鼓励感。不要写成长段说明，也不要太正式。
文案应与照片主题一致，例如可偏生活感、松弛感、元气感、励志感、可爱感、工作日常感、健身打卡感、出游快乐感。
文案字体效果以手写感为主，颜色以白色为主，重点词可以用粉色或轻微主题色强调，并可加入手绘下划线或小装饰。

【整体构图】
整体采用“中央真人主体 + 四周Q版分身环绕 + 涂鸦与短句填充留白”的结构。视觉重点仍然是真人主体，Q版分身与手绘内容负责增强趣味性与故事感。画面应丰富、有层次、适合反复观看，但整体仍然清爽、有秩序，不要变成杂乱拼贴。

【整体风格关键词】
realistic photo enhancement, chibi mini versions, kawaii sticker style, scrapbook doodles, handwritten notes, lifestyle diary aesthetic, playful and polished, soft lighting, cute but clean, high detail, social-media-friendly.

【最终效果要求】
最终成图应像一张高完成度的社交媒体生活方式手账照：真实人物自然清晰；Q版分身像本人延展出来的可爱贴纸；动作丰富、情绪多元；手绘符号与手写短句分布自然；整体呈现精致、轻松、可爱、有内容感的效果。

避免：文字乱码、Q版人物不统一、人物不像本人、元素过于拥挤、廉价拼贴感、遮挡主体、与照片主题无关的动作和文案。
```

```text
garbled text, unreadable handwriting, random unrelated words, inconsistent chibi style, chibi avatars not resembling the real person, face swap, identity change, generic influencer face, over-beautified skin, plastic skin, turning the whole real person into an illustration, destroying the original photo realism, overcrowded stickers, chaotic collage, covering the main face, covering important body areas, unrelated actions, unrelated copywriting, cheap cutout collage, mismatched lighting, messy layout
```

## Usage Notes

- This is a photo-enhancement mode, not a style diagnosis report.
- The uploaded real photo remains the hero image; chibi avatars are decorative/storytelling extensions.
- If the image tool supports reference-image input, always use the uploaded photo as the reference image to preserve likeness.
- If the model struggles with readable Chinese handwriting, reduce the number of phrases and use very short labels.
- If the original photo has little blank space, use fewer chibi stickers and place them mostly near edges.
